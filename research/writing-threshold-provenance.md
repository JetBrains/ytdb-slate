# Writing-threshold provenance

This note records only the citation trail for three public figures used by Slate's prose checker. It does not reproduce ASD-STE100 text, examples, or dictionary entries.

- **20 words:** TechScribe publishes this maximum for procedural sentences. Boeing also publishes the 20/25 pair. Sources: <https://www.simplified-english.co.uk/analysis.html#write-short-sentences> and <https://www.boeing.com/company/simplified-english-checker>.
- **25 words:** TechScribe publishes this maximum for descriptive sentences. Boeing also publishes the 20/25 pair. Sources: <https://www.simplified-english.co.uk/analysis.html#write-short-sentences> and <https://www.boeing.com/company/simplified-english-checker>.
- **Six sentences:** Boeing publishes this paragraph maximum as a checker requirement. Source: <https://www.boeing.com/company/simplified-english-checker>.

The project then cost-measured these figures against its own prose corpus. Slate applies one 20/25-word warning/failure band to all prose. It does not select a limit by text type.
